# Prospera client review

Open [the invitation-only review portal](https://betareferral.prosperaglobal.ae/auth/login). Use synthetic names, phone numbers and opportunity details. Reviewers can use a separate browser profile for each account, or sign out before switching roles.

| Review role | Sign-in email | What to try |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Platform Admin | admin@review.prospera.invalid | Inspect user controls and the service catalogue. |
| Agency Owner | owner@review.prospera.invalid | Review the agency roster and approve a member referral. |
| Agency Admin | agency-admin@review.prospera.invalid | Review a different member referral or return it for changes. |
| Agency Member | member@review.prospera.invalid | Save a draft, submit it and revisit its timeline. |
| Independent Agent | agent@review.prospera.invalid | Record activity on an assigned lead and submit a direct referral. |
| Coordinator | coordinator@review.prospera.invalid | Open an approved referral and assign the Independent Agent. |
| Corporate Referral Partner | partner@review.prospera.invalid | Submit a direct referral and follow its progress. |

The three agency accounts share Prospera Review Agency. Open **Workspace guide** on any dashboard for role-specific navigation.

## Try a referral

1. As Agency Member, open **New referral**. Choose Free Zone Company Formation and a visa package. Enter a synthetic client, phone and estimated deal value. Save a draft and record its reference.
2. Confirm the consent declaration for the synthetic scenario and submit. As Agency Owner or Agency Admin, find the reference under Referrals, choose **Inspect & Update**, then **Approve for Prospera**.
3. As Coordinator, inspect the same referral, select Independent Agent and choose **Assign referral**. As Independent Agent, add an activity note, choose the next stage and record progress.
4. Sign back in as Agency Member. Open the reference and check that the stage and activity note survived the change of session.
5. Try zero-, one- and two-visa packages, Real Estate, Mortgage, Golden Visa and Corporate Advisory. Compare direct submissions from the Independent Agent and Corporate Referral Partner with the member approval flow. Try continuing with an empty client name and submitting without consent.

## Review limits and support

Email delivery, live CRM synchronization, real property publication, contract acceptance and payouts are disabled. Any illustrative property cards are examples. Financial figures in review referrals represent synthetic inputs.

Send feedback to the Prospera contact who provided your invitation. Include the role, page, referral reference, device and browser, steps taken, expected result and actual result. Attach a screenshot with personal information removed. For a sign-in or access problem, include the time and timezone and the displayed error. Keep passwords and session tokens out of support messages.

CRM delivery, outbound email, real property publication, contract acceptance, withdrawals and payments are outside this review. Property examples are illustrative. Use the recorded referral timeline to track progress. Keep real client details, documents, bank information and money out of this environment.

Send feedback through the person who distributed your invitation. Include your role, referral reference, device or browser, steps taken, expected result and actual result. For a sign-in problem, include the time and time zone. Crop screenshots to the issue and omit passwords, session tokens and personal information.

## Credential handoff for the release owner

After final release approval, verify each recipient through the existing client contact channel. Retrieve the assigned account from the root-only beta credential file on the approved VPS and share only that account through an access-controlled password manager with a recipient-specific expiring link. Send the portal link and role separately. Keep the master file on the server. Report a misdirected invitation immediately so the operator can revoke sessions and rotate that account.
